CRMtree

CRMtree is a system for sales and account management teams. Salespeople take leads from the first contact to a signed deal, plan tasks and meetings, and look after the partner once the deal is won. Sales managers follow team results and partner sales in reports. Projects, documents, prospect enrichment and call analysis complete the picture, all in one place, in the browser and in the mobile app.

Day-to-day work of salespeople and managers: leads, partners, calendar, reports, projects, documents, prospects and call analysis.

Introduction

CRMtree is a system for sales teams and the people who work alongside them. In one place you take leads from first contact to a signed contract, look after your partners (customers), plan meetings and tasks, and, once the deal is done, run delivery projects and document workflows. Sales managers get lead and partner reports, and AI modules help you find and score new companies and review sales calls.

CRM

Leads, the lead card, activities and tasks, the activity calendar, the partner registry, onboarding.

Manager reporting

Sales dashboard, lead reports, partner performance and analytics, budgets, churn risk.

Projects

Tasks, Gantt schedule, chat, attachments and project finance.

Documents

Document register, invoices and approval workflows.

Prospects

AI enrichment of company data and scoring against your ideal customer profile.

Call analysis

AI review of sales calls and follow-up tasks after the call.

Modules are switched on for your company by the CRMtree team. If a menu item described here is missing, your company does not use that module or you do not have access to it.

Browser and mobile app

CRMtree runs in your browser and in the “CRMtree” Android app, available on Google Play. Both work on the same data: a lead added on your phone shows up on your computer straight away, and a task planned on your computer appears on your phone.

Who can use the mobile appOnly salespeople and sales managers can sign in to the app. All other accounts (for example an administrator without a CRM role, finance staff or external accounts) use the browser version.

Throughout this manual, content that applies to one version only is tagged: WEB for the browser app and MOBILE for the phone app. Untagged content applies to both.

Reminders and the daily summary

  • A reminder arrives by email and as a phone notification, sent to the activity owner (or to its author if there is no owner). It needs a due date. Closed activities do not send reminders, and changing the due date recalculates the reminder.
  • You also get a phone notification when someone assigns you a task or a lead. Tapping it opens the lead or partner card.
  • Daily summary: on working days at 9:00 CRMtree emails you “Your tasks”, with “Overdue”, “Due today” and “No due date” sections. It covers your tasks from leads, partners, onboarding, projects and document workflows. If you have nothing on, no email is sent.

Questions and answers

Why can't I move a lead two stages at once? By design, stages change one step at a time. Move through them in turn, or mark the lead as Lost.

Why can't I save a lead at the Qualification stage? From that stage on, the lead needs complete details. The message above the form lists what is missing.

CRMtree won't accept the tax ID. Check the format (PL followed by 10 digits). If the format is right, another lead or partner in your company already has that tax ID; search for it in the lead list or the partner registry.

A lead has disappeared from my list. Check the filters and the “Report filter” chip. The lead may also have been archived (choose the Archive stage filter) or moved to onboarding (look for it among your partners).

The “Edit” button is greyed out. The lead belongs to a salesperson outside your groups, so you can view it but not change it. If you are covering for a colleague, you get their access only for the dates of the substitution.

I don't get reminders on my phone. Sign in to the CRMtree app and allow notifications. A reminder needs a due date and goes to the activity owner.

Will a CRMtree meeting appear in my Google or Outlook calendar automatically? No. Use “Add to calendar”, or save the event that CRMtree opens when you add a meeting. If the date changes later, update your calendar yourself.

Can I add a lead with full details on my phone? The phone covers the essentials. Add the tax ID, source and other fields by editing the lead or in the browser.

Why does the phone dashboard show different numbers from the web? The two versions calculate the pipeline and wins slightly differently (see Home and Dashboard on the phone).

Partners

A partner is a customer your company already works with. The partner registry brings together CRM data (account owner, contacts, contract, activities) and sales figures from the data warehouse (turnover, margin, transactions). Partners linked to the warehouse carry a DWH badge.

As a salesperson you see your own partners, plus the partners of anyone you are currently substituting for. A sales manager sees the partners of their team. Partner reports and analytics for managers are covered in Sales manager.

WEBPartner registry

Go to “Partners” → “Partner registry”. Partners are shown as cards by default; switch between “Cards” and “Table” at the top.

  • The search box (“Search company, contact…”) looks through company names, email addresses, tax IDs, contact names and phone numbers.

Questions and answers: partners and settings

I can’t find a new customer in the partner registry. Partners in onboarding are shown only on the Onboarding board. They move to the registry once launched, with the status “Active”.

Why doesn’t this partner have any sales data? Sales data and churn risk are only available for partners linked to the data warehouse (DWH badge). On the phone you’ll see “This partner is not linked to the data warehouse.”

I can’t change a field marked DWH. These fields are synchronised from the transaction system. Correct them at the source.

I’ve been given a “Churn: …” task. What now? Get in touch with the customer, log the conversation on the partner card and close the task. The churn score drops once the partner starts ordering again.

The registry shows a risk but the card says “No churn risk”. The registry refreshes the score once a day in the morning, while the card calculates it live. They will match after the next refresh.

Will my substitute see my tasks in their calendar? No. The substitute can open your leads and partners (and the activities on their cards), but reminders and the daily digest still come to you.

I’ve lost my phone. In “My settings” → “Signed-in phones”, click “Sign out” next to that device. The app loses access within about fifteen minutes at most.

Can I change the language only on my phone? Yes: Profile → “Language” in the app. The language of emails and notifications is set in “My settings” in the web app.

  3. sends the collected data to the AI model, which answers yes or no for each ICP signal, writes a short summary and lists key contacts;
  4. calculates the score and saves the result in the row.

You can fill it in over several sessions: “Save draft” keeps your answers, “Submit survey” sends them to CRMtree. Passwords, tokens and API keys are stored encrypted and are no longer displayed once saved.

CRM parameters

The “CRM parameters” tab holds the CRM module’s dictionaries and thresholds. Each dictionary is a card with a list of values: “✕” removes a value, the “New value” field and “+ Add” add one. Save with “Save changes”.

Lead stages

The “Lead stages” card lets you rename funnel stages, add a step or remove one you don’t need. The stage code stays the same, so a new name shows up across the app while reports and lead records are untouched. An empty name goes back to the built-in one.

Stage (default)KindProbability
New, Qualification, Presentation, Offer, Negotiationfunnel steps10, 25, 50, 70, 85%
Won / Lostwon / lost100 / 0%
In onboarding, Onboardedconverted to partner100%
Archivearchive0%
  • “Add stage” adds a step at the end of the funnel, before Won. Its probability is halfway between the previous step and 100%. The funnel can have up to 10 steps.
  • “Delete” works for funnel steps and for Won and Lost. Leads on the deleted stage move to the previous step (from the first step to the next one; from Won and Lost to the last step). The confirmation tells you how many leads move and where.
  • The converted and archive stages cannot be deleted because the app sets them itself, and neither can the last funnel step. You can always rename them.

What salespeople can do: a new lead starts on the first step. From any stage a lead moves one step forward or back, or straight to Lost. From Won it can only go back to the last step; from Lost and Archive only to the first step. Moving to Lost requires a loss reason. “Hold” (pausing a lead with a reason and a date) is possible on every step except the first.

Churn and Health Score

Two tables: “Churn algorithm — scoring parameters” and “Health Score — scoring parameters”. Here you set the points for no orders (in days), sales drop or growth between the last two months, activity in the last 20 days, and the thresholds for risk levels (default 21 / 51 / 71 / 91 points) and health levels (good from 61, warning from 21). The schedule parameter (default 06:00) is the time when the server recalculates the scores every day and sends email alerts. These parameters matter when the company has partner sales data from the data warehouse. The results are described in the sales manager part.

Onboarding templates, lead sources and tooltips

The “Onboarding templates” tab has two editors.

Onboarding task templates are the standard tasks for each step of bringing a new partner on board: “Contract signing”, “Configuration”, “Training”, “Launch”. For each template you set the title, type (Call, Email, Meeting, Task, Training, Document), assignee and the number of days after the lead is migrated (the due date). A green background marks a standard task, created automatically when a lead moves to onboarding. Other templates are suggested when tasks are created in the Onboarding panel. The “Contract signing” step always goes to the lead’s salesperson.

Lead sources dictionary is the list of values for the lead’s source field: “Value (code)”, “Label (displayed)” and “Group”. Values with a group (blue background, e.g. “Marketing”) are shown in the Marketing section of lists. “Add source” adds a row.

Tooltips

The “Tooltips” tab adds explanations to the analytics screens. Choose a “Screen” (Sales Dashboard, Sales reports, Partner performance, Other), a “Label” from the list, type the “Tooltip text” and click “Add tooltip”. A “?” icon appears next to that label, and hovering over it shows your text. It’s a good place to explain how your company calculates conversion or sales cycle length.

Enrichment / ICP

The “Enrichment / ICP” tab (with the Prospects module) defines your ideal customer profile. For each company, the AI answers yes or no to every active ICP signal, and CRMtree adds up the points of the signals answered yes. The result is a score from 0 to 100 shown in the prospect list.

  • Each signal has an on/off switch, a name, a short description and points. “Edit” opens a dialog: “Signal name”, “How to recognise this signal?” (the instruction for the AI, passed on word for word), “Tooltip / short description for the user”, “Points”, “Active”.
  • “+ Add signal” adds a new signal.
  • The points of the active signals must add up to exactly 100. Only then does CRMtree publish a new version of the configuration. Until that, enrichment keeps using the last valid version.
  • The “Last update” line shows the version number, date and author. A signal that has already been used is only switched off when you delete it, so older results can still be explained.

Final score = points of the “yes” signals minus the blacklist penalty (parameters on the “CRM parameters” tab), kept within 0–100. The “→ Lead” button becomes active from the “Prospects — minimum score for conversion to Lead” threshold.

EXAMPLE · SCORING A COMPANY

Demo setup: “Dział handlowy” (sales department) 25, “Indywidualna wycena” (custom pricing) 25, “Konsultacja / demo” 15, “Dedykowana opieka” (dedicated account care) 15, “Przetargi” (tenders) 5, “Sieć partnerów” (partner network) 5 and others. Conversion threshold 45. The AI confirmed a sales department, consultations and tenders.

Score = 25 + 15 + 5 = 45, so “→ Lead” is active. If the company’s industry contained a blacklisted keyword (e.g. wholesale, “hurtow”), the score would drop by 15 points to 30 and conversion would not be available.

KSeF — cost invoices

KSeF is the Polish national e-invoicing system.

  1. Switch on project finance. The “KSeF — cost invoices” card appears with an environment badge (production or test).
  2. “Add company”: tax ID (NIP), name (optional) and the KSeF token generated for that tax ID. CRMtree checks the token with KSeF before saving; afterwards only its last four characters are shown.
  3. Set “First sync goes back (days)” (1–365, default 30).
  4. Choose the “Access group for invoice documents”, e.g. “Księgowość”. Invoices linked to projects are then registered in the Documents module, visible to that group’s members.
  5. Give the people who link invoices the “Access to KSeF invoices” permission on their user card.

Company status: “Active”, “Token rejected” (sync paused until you enter a new token with “Change”) or “Sync error” (retried on the next run). “Sync now” starts a download immediately.

Telephony and the call analyser

“PBX numbers” (with the telephony module) assigns each salesperson their Personal Access Token from the ip-pbx.eu phone system. Once the token is saved, CRMtree fetches the SIP details and the direct phone number (DPN), and the salesperson can call from the softphone in the browser.

  1. Find the user in the list (columns: User, CRM role, Direct number (DPN), Status, Updated).
  2. Click “Set token” and paste the token into “Personal Access Token (PAT)”. The salesperson creates the token in the ip-pbx.eu panel: avatar in the top right corner → API or Personal Access Token → create and copy the token.
  3. After saving, the status changes to “Configured” and the number appears in the DPN column.

Salespeople can also enter their own token in “My settings”. “Delete” removes the token, and the person can no longer use the softphone.

The call analyser (Call analysis module) needs three things:

  • an active AI key in the “Call analysis” row (AI providers);
  • the “Call analyser” box ticked under “Feature access” on the card of everyone who should use it;
  • a source of notes: softphone calls (transcripts from the phone system) or a CSV file of call notes imported on the “Call analysis” screen.

CSV import and export

Administration → “CSV import”. The “Sales leads”, “Partners” and “Documents (metadata)” cards list their columns (required ones marked *, dictionary columns with a hover hint) and offer “Download template”, “Export data” and a “Drag a CSV file here or click” drop zone.

RuleDetails
File.csv or .txt, up to 10 MB, UTF-8. The separator (“;” or “,”) is detected automatically. The first row is the header.
NumbersSpaces are ignored; both “1.000,50” and “1,000.50” are understood. A probability of 0.4 means 40%.
LeadsColumn company is required. stage takes the stage code (e.g. qualification), not its name. An empty stage means the first funnel step. assigned_to_email sets the owner (otherwise it’s the person importing). Row limit: the “CSV import row limit” parameter.
PartnersColumn company is required. partner_number links the partner to sales data, group_name is the partner group, manager_email the account owner.
DocumentsColumn name is required. Type, GDPR type and status must be dictionary codes. Upload the files themselves after the import.

After an import, the result panel shows how many rows were imported, skipped and failed, with an error table by row number. “Import history” lists recent imports with who ran them and the result.

Imports add new recordsEvery row creates a new record, even if the company is already in the CRM. Don’t import the same file twice. Custom fields are filled in on the cards.

Export (“Export data”) downloads a CSV file with the template headers: leads (except those converted to partners), partners or document metadata.

Implementation checklist

  1. Fill in and submit the implementation survey. CRMtree switches on your modules and sets up email, WhatsApp and telephony.
  2. Global parameters: default language, document thresholds, import limit, email signature. Check “Training mode” and switch it off before going live.
  3. AI providers (Prospects, call analyser, mobile form assistant, SEObot): a key and model for each feature, status “active”.
  4. CRM parameters: lead stages, loss and hold reasons, industries, job titles, currencies, partner statuses, commission bases, and a decision on “Global CRM read access”.
  5. Custom fields for leads, partners and documents.
  6. Lead sources and onboarding task templates.
  7. Document dictionaries: types, statuses, GDPR, payment statuses, entities, contract subjects.
  8. Groups matching your teams and departments, with owner restriction where needed. Partner groups.
  9. Users: accounts, CRM roles, passwords handed over in person, group memberships (every sales manager in at least one group), visibility grants, access to Prospects and the call analyser, project and KSeF permissions.
  10. Budgets for salespeople (administrator or sales manager).
  11. PBX tokens for salespeople, if you use telephony.
  12. Prospects: ICP signals adding up to 100 points, the lead conversion threshold, blacklist keywords.
  13. Projects: statuses and categories, transition matrix, types, priorities, task fields, at-risk threshold, finance, cost categories, KSeF companies and the invoice documents group.
  14. Tooltips for the analytics screens (optional).
  15. Data import: download the templates, import partners first, then leads, then documents. Check the import history and fix any errors.
  16. Mobile app: salespeople and sales managers install “CRMtree” from Google Play and sign in with the same email and password.
  17. Configuration backup: Data management → “Download settings.json”.
  18. Check the audit logs: settings changes, new accounts, role assignments, imports.
